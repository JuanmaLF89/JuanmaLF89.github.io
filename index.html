How it works

Four steps, nothing new to sign up for.

  1. Type the cards

    One per line or separated by commas, with the quantity in front if you want several. Card names work in your language, even incomplete.

  2. Pick the version

    I show you what I have of each card: edition, language, condition and foil. Tap the one you want.

  3. Get the link

    I prepare the order on Cardmarket and within minutes you get a link with your reserved cards.

  4. Buy on Cardmarket

    Add them to your cart and pay on Cardmarket, with its usual buyer protection. Payment never goes through Telegram.

Buying rules

  1. Purchase and payment on Cardmarket

    The assistant only prepares the order. Purchase and payment always happen on Cardmarket.

  2. 72 h reservation

    The cards you pick are reserved for you. Once you get the link you have 72 h to buy them; after that they may go back on sale.

  3. Card condition

    I grade the condition (EX, GD, LP…) myself when I list the cards. When you order, I ask whether you accept a slightly different final condition; if you say no and a card doesn't match, I'll tell you before shipping.

  4. Shipping

    Cardmarket gives me up to 7 days to ship, although I usually ship sooner. If you need it fast, type /urgent.

  5. Cancelling

    Until you buy it, you can cancel the order or remove cards with /cancel. Once paid, cancellations go through Cardmarket.

Frequently asked questions

Does the assistant cost anything?

No. The assistant is free: you only pay for the cards and shipping on Cardmarket, like any other purchase there.

Is it safe?

Payment never goes through Telegram or the assistant. You buy directly on Cardmarket, with its payment system and buyer protection.

Do I need a Cardmarket account?

Yes. The link you get opens your cards on Cardmarket, and you need your account there to buy them.

Which cards can I order?

The ones in my collection. I have many more cards than you can see listed in my Cardmarket store, and the assistant searches all of them. If I don't have one, it tells you right away.

How long does the link take?

Usually a few minutes. The assistant messages you in the same chat when your order is ready.

What if I don't buy in the end?

No problem. The reservation lasts 72 h from when you get the link; after that the cards may go back on sale. You can also cancel earlier with /cancel.

How do I change the language?

The assistant uses your Telegram language. To change it, type /language.

Is this an official Cardmarket service?

No. It's the assistant of my Cardmarket store (Jumperial). It is not affiliated with Cardmarket or Wizards of the Coast.
